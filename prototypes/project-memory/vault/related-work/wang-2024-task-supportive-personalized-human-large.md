# Task-supportive ChatGPT interaction (Wang et al., 2024)

## Summary
A small user study (16 people) of a ChatGPT interface with prompt suggestions and explanations. Indirect: no output metrics, but one case shows a user staying satisfied despite wrong outputs once their expectations shifted.

## Relevant findings
- **Satisfaction can stay high despite wrong outputs when users adjust their expectations.**

> [!quote]- Source: Wang et al. 2024, p. 7
> "However, inconsistencies arose when P1 increased the rule complexity. After reviewing the conversation explanations, P1 adjusted their expectations, acknowledging ChatGPT’s limitations but remaining satisfied."
>
> [[sources/wang-2024-task-supportive-personalized-human-large.pdf]] · https://doi.org/10.1145/3627508.3638344

## Connection to our investigations
One reason [[interaction-quality]] may not track [[ai-output-quality]] closely in [[output-vs-interaction-quality-crossword]]: expectations shape satisfaction.
