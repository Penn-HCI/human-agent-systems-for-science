# Heuristic evaluation of conversational agents (Langevin et al., 2021)

## Summary
Proposes usability heuristics for chatbots and voice assistants. Answer: indirect, with no data on output metrics. It argues that a good experience depends on the interaction as well as on what the agent says.

## Relevant findings
- **Judging a conversational agent's experience means looking at the interaction, not only the conversation content.**

> [!quote]- Source: Langevin et al. 2021, p. 9
> "Providing a good user experience may require an evaluation of the conversation as well as user interactions."
>
