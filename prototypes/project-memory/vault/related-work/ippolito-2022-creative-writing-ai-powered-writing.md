# Wordcraft with professional writers (Ippolito et al., 2022)

## Summary
An interview and diary study of 13 professional writers using an AI writing tool. Answer: indirect, with no measurements. Writers say the qualities the model was tuned for, such as sensibleness and safety, are not always what they want.

## Relevant findings
- **The output qualities the model was tuned for (safe, sensible) don't match what writers need.**

> [!quote]- Source: Ippolito et al. 2022, p. 12
> "It is evident that the goals of a safe bot for chitchat conversations are not perfectly aligned with the goals of a creative writing assistant. Similarity, groundedness and sensibility are not always desirable features."
