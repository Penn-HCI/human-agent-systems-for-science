# Biased AI writing assistants shift attitudes (Williams-Ceci et al., 2026)

## Summary
Two experiments (N = 2582) where biased autocomplete suggestions shifted writers' attitudes. Indirect: users' own ratings of the suggestions missed the bias, so perceived output quality hid a harmful effect.

## Relevant findings
- **Most users rated biased suggestions as balanced; only 14–19% disagreed.**

> [!quote]- Source: Williams-Ceci et al. 2026, p. 4
> "Most participants exposed to the biased AI suggestions in the AI Treatment across both experiments agreed that the AI suggestions “were reasonable and balanced,” indicating a lack of awareness of the suggestions’ bias. As shown in Fig. 4, only 19% of participants in experiment 1 and 14% of participants in experiment 2 disagreed with the statement that the suggestions were balanced."
>
