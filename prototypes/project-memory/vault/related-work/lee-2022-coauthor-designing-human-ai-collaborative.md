# CoAuthor (Lee et al., 2022)

## Summary
A dataset of 1,445 writing sessions with GPT-3 plus post-task surveys. It doesn't test the question directly (indirect); how much text GPT-3 contributed relates a little to ownership and not to satisfaction.

## Relevant findings
- **The more of the text writers wrote themselves, the more ownership they felt (r = 0.3), but not the more satisfied they were (r = 0.0–0.1).**

> [!quote]- Source: Lee et al. 2022, p. 10
> "For ownership scores (rated as a 5-point Likert scale) and the fraction of text written by writers, the Pearson correlation coefficient was 0.3 in both creative and argumentative writing, whereas it was 0.1 and 0.0 for satisfaction scores."
>
> [[sources/lee-2022-coauthor-designing-human-ai-collaborative.pdf]] · https://doi.org/10.1145/3491102.3502030
