# Human-centered evaluation and auditing of LMs (Xiao et al., 2024)

## Summary
A CHI workshop proposal on human-centered evaluation of LLMs. Answer: indirect, with no data. It notes that neither benchmarks nor output ratings have been shown to predict real-world usefulness.

## Relevant findings
- **Rating outputs, by experts or crowdworkers, is criticized as not valid for judging real-world usefulness.**

> [!quote]- Source: Xiao et al. 2024, p. 2
> "In practice, some forms of human evaluation such as rating outputs by domain experts or crowd workers are often used to evaluate language models. However, these practices are largely ad-hoc and have been widely criticized for lacking standardization, reproducibility, and validity for assessing model utility in real-world settings [6]."
