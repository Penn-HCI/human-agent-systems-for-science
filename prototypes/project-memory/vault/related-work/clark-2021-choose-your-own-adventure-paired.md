# Choose Your Own Adventure (Clark et al., 2021)

## Summary
Writers pick between two models' suggestions while writing a story. It doesn't test the question directly (indirect), but the model writers preferred in use scored worse on an automatic repetition metric.

## Relevant findings
- **Writers chose GPT-2 over Fusion 66% of the time even though GPT-2 was more repetitive (distinct-1 0.53 vs. 0.75); its share fell from 76% to 57% over the story, which the authors put down to the repetition.**

> [!quote]- Source: Clark et al. 2021, p. 4
> "From Table 3, we see that GPT 2 generates shorter, more concrete, and more repetitive suggestions than FUSION. [...] GPT 2 generated the most repetitive text, which may explain why it is chosen less frequently as the story goes on."
>
