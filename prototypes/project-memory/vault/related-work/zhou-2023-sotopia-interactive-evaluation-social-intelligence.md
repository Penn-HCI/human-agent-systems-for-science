# SOTOPIA (Zhou et al., 2023)

## Summary
A role-play environment for testing agents' social skills, with GPT-4 rating episodes against human raters. Indirect: it compares an LLM judge with third-party human ratings, not participants' own experience.

## Relevant findings
- **GPT-4 ratings track human ratings well for goal completion (r = 0.71–0.78), but weakly on most other dimensions, especially when a human plays the character.**

> [!quote]- Source: Zhou et al. 2023, p. 6
> "The correlations show that when models are role-playing, the GPT-4 scores have significant and strong correlations with the humans' scores on GOAL, FIN, and REL dimensions. However, when humans are role-playing, the correlations drop significantly on all but one dimension (GOAL)."
>
