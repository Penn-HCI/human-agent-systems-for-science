# Co-writing with opinionated language models (Jakesch et al., 2023)

## Summary
An experiment where a writing assistant was set to argue one side of a debate. It doesn't test the question directly (indirect); users rated the assistant well whether or not its slant matched their own views.

## Relevant findings
- **Users' ratings of the assistant barely reflected its bias: most said it was knowledgeable even when it argued against them.**

> [!quote]- Source: Jakesch et al. 2023, p. 11
> "The vast majority of participants thought the language model had expertise and was knowledgeable – even if it contradicted their personal views."
>
> [[sources/jakesch-2023-co-writing-opinionated-language-models.pdf]] · https://doi.org/10.1145/3544548.3581196
